BURGER HAVEN — MANUAL GCASH & MAYA PAYMENT SETUP
================================================

This project DOES NOT use a payment API, payment gateway, or automatic wallet verification.
Customers send money directly to your official GCash or Maya account. You check your actual
wallet transaction and manually approve or reject their payment claim in the staff dashboard.
Cash on Delivery stays available as before.

LOCAL SETUP (Windows)
--------------------
1. Start Burger Haven once using START_BURGER_HAVEN.bat. It creates Burger_Haven_App\.env
   from .env.example if .env does not exist.
2. Stop the running server by clicking its terminal and pressing Ctrl+C.
3. Open Burger_Haven_App\.env in Notepad or VS Code.
4. Find these settings and fill in your own official receiving account information:

   GCASH_ACCOUNT_NAME=Your official GCash account name
   GCASH_NUMBER=Your GCash receiving mobile number
   MAYA_ACCOUNT_NAME=Your official Maya account name
   MAYA_NUMBER=Your Maya receiving mobile number

   Replace the example text with your real authorized account details. Keep the values on one
   line. Do not enter any PIN, password, or OTP. Keep the QR variables blank unless you add
   a merchant QR image in Burger_Haven_App\static\ and use its /static/... path.

5. Save .env and start START_BURGER_HAVEN.bat again.
6. Open http://127.0.0.1:8000 and place a test order selecting GCash or Maya.
   The order screen should display the configured account name, number, order reference, and
   exact total amount. If a wallet is not configured, do not accept payment through it.

CUSTOMER PAYMENT FLOW
---------------------
1. The customer places an order and selects GCash or Maya.
2. Burger Haven displays your configured account name/number and exact amount to send.
3. The customer sends the money using their own wallet app, then enters the transaction reference.
   They may also upload a JPG, PNG, or WEBP screenshot up to 5 MB.
4. The payment status becomes "Awaiting Verification".
5. In http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin, inspect the order total and claim. Open your actual GCash/Maya
   account and verify that the exact amount was received from the customer.
6. Click "Approve verified payment" only after confirming the incoming transaction. If it is not
   correct or cannot be found, reject the claim with a clear reason.
7. Cash/Cash on Delivery orders continue through the existing cash-collection flow.

IMPORTANT
---------
- A receipt screenshot or reference number alone does not prove money was received.
- Never mark a transfer paid unless you have checked the actual account transaction.
- Configure the same four account variables in your hosting provider's Environment/Variables
  settings when deploying online; do not publish .env or real account details in GitHub.
- Set a new strong ADMIN_TOKEN before public deployment and use HTTPS.
